## FAQ

#### Do you have any hotel recommendations?

Our preferred hotel is:

- **[Novotel Tianjin Meijiang](https://all.accor.com/hotel/B697/index.en.shtml)** (天津梅江诺富特酒店), the venue for evening activities and streamed sessions, approximately **10 minutes by taxi** from Peiyangyuan Campus.

The special room rate is **RMB 380**. Please contact the hotel in advance to receive this rate.

Additional recommended hotels:

- [Ji Hotel (Jinnan University Town Weishan South Road)](https://hotels.ctrip.com/hotels/80335508.html) — 全季酒店（津南大学城微山南路店）.
- [Vienna Hotel (Shuanggang Metro Station)](https://hotels.ctrip.com/hotels/7840542.html) — 维也纳酒店（双港地铁店）.

The taxi time is approximate and depends on traffic and the campus entrance used. Allow extra time during peak hours.
