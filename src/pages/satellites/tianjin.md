Tianjin Satellite
======

**November 10-13, 2026**

## Place

[Tianjin University, Peiyangyuan Campus](https://www.tju.edu.cn/tdgk/tdxq/byyxq.htm),<br>
135 Yaguan Road, Haihe Education Park,<br>
Jinnan District, Tianjin, China

For attendees' convenience, evening activities will take place at both Tianjin Scientists & Engineers Club and Peiyangyuan Campus.

## Program

The local program is being finalized. Details of local talks, posters, and activities will be announced as they are confirmed.

All times below are in **Beijing Time (UTC+8)**. Evening sessions follow the Boston conference schedule. The 23:00-00:30 sessions end on the following calendar day; the final session ends at 00:30 on Saturday, November 14.

"China VIS Meetup" refers to **VIS Meetup: Opportunities and Development in China**.

## FAQ

#### Do you have any hotel recommendations?

Daytime activities will take place at Tianjin University's Peiyangyuan Campus. We recommend the following nearby hotel:

- [Tianjin Scientists & Engineers Club](https://hk.trip.com/hotels/tianjin-hotel-detail-17534476/tianjin-scientists-engineers-club/) (天津科技工作者之家), approximately 3.6 km from the campus, with an estimated 10-15-minute taxi ride.

Other nearby hotel options include:

- [Crowne Plaza Tianjin Jinnan](https://www.ihg.com/crowneplaza/hotels/us/en/tianjin/tsntj/hoteldetail) (天津京基皇冠假日酒店), approximately 2.3 km from the campus, with an estimated 10-15-minute taxi ride.
- [Novotel Tianjin Meijiang](https://all.accor.com/hotel/B697/index.en.shtml) (天津梅江诺富特酒店), approximately 4.5 km from the campus, with an estimated 15-20-minute taxi ride.
- [Hampton by Hilton Tianjin Haihe Education Park NCEC](https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/tsnthhx-hampton-tianjin-haihe-education-park-ncec/) (天津海教园国家会展中心希尔顿欢朋酒店), approximately 5 km from the campus, with an estimated 15-20-minute taxi ride.
