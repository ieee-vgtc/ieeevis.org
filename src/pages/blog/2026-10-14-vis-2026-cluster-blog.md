IEEE VIS 2026 is excited to announce [Cluster](https://cluster-practitioners-summit.github.io/), the inaugural Practitioners' Summit, an event that brings together data visualization freelancers and industry professionals with academics and scientific researchers to encourage cross-pollination of ideas and the crafting of deeper collaborations. Cluster: The Practitioners' Summit will present a host of programming, including an FAQ event for attendees who are curious about beginning a career in data visualization freelancing. Check out the full programming below!

## Keynote: Data Viz Isn't a Chart You Drop In at the End

<img src="/year/2026/assets/posts/2026-10-cluster/sophie-sparkes.png" alt="A woman with short, dark hair smiling in front of white orchids." width="200" />
<br />

Cluster will kick off the morning with a quick, 10-minute keynote from **Sophie Sparkes** discussing how practitioners can make the case for data visualisation as a design discipline, not an afterthough. Sophie is a Data Experience Designer and PhD researcher at the Diverse CDT, City St George's University of London, with over 13 years in data visualization practice.

## Live Policy Viz Podcast

<img src="/year/2026/assets/posts/2026-10-cluster/panel-headshots.png" alt="Our three panelists' headshots show a black man in a light blue collared shirt, a white woman in a gray and black dress and yellow glasses, and a white man in wire-frame glasses and a brown tweed suit. All three are smiling at the camera in front of professional backdrops." width="600" />
<br />

Next, Cluster will host a live Policy Viz podcast in discussion over AI's role in visualization practice, research, and the job market. Hosted by **Jonathan Schwabish**, our three panelists, **Yannick Assogba**, **Amber Thomas**, and **Doug Elmendorf**, will bring their extensive experience in visualization and AI industry research, visualization consulting, visualization engineering, and public policy, to the stage to weigh in on how AI does and does not impact vis career trajectories in industry and freelancing. The podcast taping will be punctuated by a coffee break, followed by a live Q&A session.

## Freelancing FAQ

Cluster will wrap up with a Freelancing FAQ session, to advise curious attendees on how to start and build a successful visualization consulting business. This FAQ session will cover a range of topics, including negotiating rates, legal considerations (in the US), building a brand, marketing one's business, and finding clients. This session will have multiple tables, each focusing on specific FAQ areas, so that everyone from freelance-curious to current business owners can benefit from the discussion. We are still soliciting FAQs from the community – [add yours here](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1cX5rG4ZOBwcZSjZdqAL2RBMRkw6ph1jhPWEwYDI0sx0/edit?usp=sharing). Come learn about self-employment as an alternative or supplement to industry and academic careers!

## Anonymous Data Vis Draft Feedback

<img src="/year/2026/assets/posts/2026-10-cluster/crit-example-setup.png" style="display: block; margin: 0 auto;" alt="Illustration of two attendees viewing printed visualization drafts posted on a wall, each with a feedback box beneath it. Artwork by Racquel Fygenson from Eyelight Labs." width="600" />

We are also collecting data visualization drafts for anonymous, asynchronous feedback from the community throughout the day. Drafts will be printed and posted around the coffee chat and lunch area for the duration of Cluster. Drafts can be anonymous, or list your name. This initiative is a great way to prompt converstion, display interesting work, and get valueable opinions on your latest vis endeavors. [Submit your drafts here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScfZI4MhSME-aqGZbKG_Y_Oy6c92rwxMsv0pBMOC0oJYujauQ/viewform?usp=header).
