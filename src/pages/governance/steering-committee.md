The task of the IEEE VIS Steering Committee (VSC) is to provide long-term scientific and organizational oversight of IEEE VIS. The mission statement of the VSC can be [found here](/year/2026/governance/vsc-mission-statement).

## VIS Executive and Steering Committee Elections

Each year the VIS community elects one new member each to the VEC and VSC.
See the [VIS Elections](/year/2026/info/elections) page for more details.
